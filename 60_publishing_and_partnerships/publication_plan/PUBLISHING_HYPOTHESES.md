# Publishing Hypotheses

Status: Hypotheses only.

## Possible Volume Logic

- Regional volumes.
- Technique volumes: oven, fire, preservation, pasta, pastry, sauces.
- Ingredient volumes: grain, oil, wine, meat, fruit, chocolate.
- Function volumes: daily, medical, ritual, feast, preservation.
- Historical period volumes.
- Production process volumes: oil, wine, bread, cheese.

## Current Recommendation

Do not choose the book series structure until the source inventory and taxonomy reveal where the strongest clusters are.
